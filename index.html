Why We Built on Robinhood

The rails were already there.

Robinhood Chain is a permissionless, Ethereum-compatible L2 — built for tokenized equities, AI-agent trading, and on-chain settlement. We studied the market structure for weeks before choosing it.

01

Tokenized Equities Are Real

Tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain are the actual equities market moving on-chain. This isn't a synthetic wrapper — these are real assets with real market structure.

02

AI Agents Need Infrastructure

An AI model that can analyze, decide, and execute trades needs a unified layer — not a patchwork of broker APIs. We built that layer directly on Robinhood Chain.

03

Governance Matters at Scale

When AI agents trade autonomously, governance isn't optional. Every decision needs audit trails, risk controls, and kill switches. Teresa enforces that natively.

04

One Layer for Every Provider

OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Llama — each has its own interface. Teresa normalizes them into a single routing layer so your strategy runs across any model.

Tokenized Equities

The market structure we studied.

Before writing a line of code, we mapped where tokenized equities actually stand — who's building, where liquidity sits, and what's missing at the infrastructure layer.

Robinhood Chain is live

A permissionless L2 built on Arbitrum technology, purpose-built for tokenized stocks and AI-agent execution.

AI agents lack a control layer

Existing infrastructure lets agents execute trades but provides no unified routing, governance, or auditability across providers.

The gap is infrastructure, not models

The AI models that can trade profitably already exist. What's missing is the plumbing that connects them safely to real markets.

That's why we built Teresa

One routing layer. Full governance. Complete control. Built natively on Robinhood for tokenized equity trading.

What We're Building

Running on the rails.

The Robinhood Chain is the infrastructure. Teresa is the intelligence layer that makes AI-driven trading possible, safe, and auditable on top of it.

AI Trading Router

One SDK that routes your trading signals across every major AI provider — with automatic failover, load balancing, and cost optimization built in.

teresa.route(signal, { providers: ['gpt-4o', 'claude'] })

Governance Engine

Define and enforce risk parameters, spend limits, allowed tickers, and compliance rules across every AI agent — automatically.

teresa.govern({ maxSpend: 500, riskProfile: 'conservative' })

Robinhood Execution

Direct integration with Robinhood Chain for on-chain settlement of tokenized equities — no intermediaries, full audit trail on every trade.

teresa.execute(signal, { chain: 'robinhood' })

Real-Time Observability

Live dashboards tracking every AI decision, latency per provider, alpha generation per model, and a complete audit log — all in real time.

teresa.observe({ stream: true, metrics: 'all' })

Kill Switch Controls

Instant override capability — pause any agent, block any provider, or halt all trading activity with a single call. Full control, always.

teresa.control(agentId, { action: 'halt' })

Open Source

Built in the open. Inspect the routing logic, audit the governance engine, or fork it for your own stack. AI trading infrastructure for everyone.
